What is a Lead Magnate?

A lead magnet is a valuable piece of educational content offered to potential customers in exchange for their contact information. Businesses use lead magnets to build trust, demonstrate expertise, and generate qualified leads by providing helpful resources that solve a specific problem or answer a common question.

Portfolio Notice: These materials were created as part of a digital marketing internship and are displayed solely for portfolio and educational purposes. Please do not contact any businesses using information contained within these samples.

Home Services Lead Magnet Suite

This project involved creating a collection of educational lead magnets for home service companies. The goal was to provide homeowners with useful, actionable information while supporting customer acquisition and lead generation efforts.

Project Overview

The lead magnets were developed as downloadable resources designed to attract potential customers, build trust, and demonstrate subject-matter expertise. Each document addressed a common homeowner concern and provided practical guidance in an easy-to-read format.

Resources Created

The collection included The Homeowner's Guide to Rodent-Proofing Your NC House, 21 Winter Plumbing Mistakes Homeowners Make, and 7 Signs Your HVAC System Is About to Fail This Winter. Each guide combined educational content with strategic marketing objectives designed to support customer engagement and lead generation.

Skills Demonstrated

This project strengthened my abilities in content strategy, digital marketing, customer education, copywriting, document design, lead generation, and creating content that balances user value with business goals.

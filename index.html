About Me

Every system reveals itself in its anomalies — the quirks and unexpected outcomes that open the door to deeper understanding and better solutions.

My career path may look different from that of a traditional technology candidate. Most of my professional experience has been in self-employment, caregiving, and hands-on service work. However, those years taught me skills that many employers value deeply: reliability, accountability, communication, problem-solving, adaptability, and the ability to work independently without supervision.

I live in rural western North Carolina in the small community of Casar. In areas like mine, opportunities can be limited, and many residents either create their own work or commute long distances for employment. As a result, I spent much of my career building and maintaining my own client base through self-employment, where my reputation depended entirely on trust, consistency, and results.


Technology has always been more than a career interest for me. It has been a long-standing passion and hobby. In 2023, I made the decision to formally transition that passion into a professional career. Since then, I have completed an Associate in Applied Science in Programming & Web Development, graduating with Summa Cum Laude honors and maintaining a 4.0 GPA throughout my coursework. I also earned multiple IT certificates in Web Development, Webmaster, Social Media, Computer Science Foundations, and IT Foundations.


My technical background includes HTML, CSS, JavaScript, responsive web design, WordPress development, SEO, content strategy, digital marketing, Git, and GitHub. During a five-month remote internship with Epitome Digital Marketing, I worked on client-facing projects involving website development, search engine optimization, email and SMS marketing campaigns, social media content creation, and lead-generation assets. This experience strengthened my ability to create digital solutions that balance usability, accessibility, and business goals.


One of my proudest projects is AIForgeRun.com, a website that I designed, developed, host, and maintain independently. Through the site, I publish practical guides, reviews, and educational content focused on artificial intelligence, large language models, web technologies, and emerging software platforms. Managing every aspect of the project has allowed me to strengthen my skills in development, SEO, content creation, hosting, and digital marketing while staying current with rapidly evolving technology.


I am particularly passionate about AI, automation, and the future of intelligent digital experiences. I enjoy understanding how systems work, identifying opportunities for improvement, and building solutions that make processes more efficient. I believe technology is most powerful when it augments human creativity and productivity, allowing people to focus on higher-level thinking, innovation, and meaningful work.


While my professional technology experience is still growing, my work ethic is not. I have built my career by showing up every day, maintaining strong client relationships, treating people with respect, and taking pride in producing quality work. I am eager to bring that same dedication, persistence, and willingness to learn to a remote team where I can contribute meaningful value and continue developing as a technology professional.


I may not have followed the traditional path into technology, but I have done the work to earn my place in it. All I am looking for is an opportunity to demonstrate what I can contribute. I believe many employers would be pleasantly surprised by the commitment, adaptability, and determination I bring to every project and role.
